## 2. Has this been done before?

**Short answer: the pieces have, the loop has not. Nobody has closed it, at any price.**

The category is crowded and consolidating fast, but every player breaks at one of two joints.

### The landscape, honestly

**Creative generators (render farms).** Arcads ($16M seed Dec 2025, 6,000+ clients, profitable), Creatify ($24M raised, $9M ARR), AdCreative.ai (acquired by Appier for $38.7M), Creatopy, Omneky, Pencil, Topview.ai, MakeUGC. Independent 2026 comparisons note that **none of the pure UGC tools connect to ad-account performance data**. They generate. They do not know if it worked.

**Creative intelligence (analysis only).** Motion ($30M Series B, $750 to $1,200/mo), Foreplay ($59 to $459/mo), Atria (trained on $9B+ spend, mines competitor hooks and landing pages), Vidmob ($200M+ raised, launched Vidmob360 MCP services June 2026), CreativeX, Marpipe. These read performance and produce briefs. **Motion generates zero creative and runs zero campaigns.** MagicBrief, the best-known one, was bought by Canva and **shut down 31 July 2026**.

**Media buying agents.** Smartly.io ($7B+ spend under management, launched Synapse orchestration layer June 2026, but a ~€5,000/mo floor and 6 to 12 week implementation), Madgicx, Revealbot, Hunch. Plus the platforms themselves: Meta Advantage+ (Zuckerberg's stated goal is advertising that needs only "a credit card number and a business goal" by end-2026) and Google Performance Max.

**The closest thing to your spec that exists:** **Uplane** (YC F25, ~$4.5M raised, 22 people). Analyzes audience, brand style and competitor archives, generates thousands of ads **and matching landing pages**, one-click launch to Meta/Google/LinkedIn/TikTok/Taboola, algorithmic budget steering, learns for profit not just ROAS. Then **Creatify** (real cross-platform buying agent under a real creative engine), **Canva Grow 2.0** (launched at Cannes June 2026, cleanest three-platform loop, but no Google Ads), **Atria**, **GetCrux** (YC W24, best data breadth).

And a cautionary tale worth knowing before you pitch: **Icon (icon.com), the literal "AI CMO,"** raised $9.2M from Founders Fund, spent $12M on the domain, and **shut down by early 2026**, pivoting to selling human-made UGC ads at $399. The architecture was right. The output quality was not.

### The two joints nobody has closed

**Joint A: asset-type selection.** No product on the market decides *what kind of marketing to run*. Every tool assumes the format is already given. Creatify assumes video. Marpipe assumes catalog statics. Unbounce assumes the problem is the page. Atria assumes the problem is the ad. **Not one system says "your creative is fine, your landing page is the leak" or "stop making statics, this audience needs UGC."** That decision is one hundred percent human, everywhere, at every price point.

**Joint B: strategy origination.** Canva Grow says it out loud in its own positioning: the offer, the hook and the audience thesis stay human. Smartly Synapse requires "marketer intent" as an input. Meta's official Ads MCP server lands agent-created campaigns **paused**. Google PMax requires asset review. Every agent optimizes *within* a strategy. None authors one.

### The wedge that is actually yours

Here is the part that matters, and it is not on anyone's roadmap.

**Every system in this category runs on your historical account performance.** Motion needs your ad history. Creatify's media buyer ingests your account history. Canva Grow syncs performance back. Atria grades your ads after they run. Smartly optimizes what you already spend.

**A brand launching next week has none of that.** Zero impressions, zero conversions, zero creative history. Every incumbent is cold-start blind, and the cold start is exactly when the decision matters most, because that is when you pick the market, the language, the offer, the format and the funnel shape, and those choices set the ceiling on everything after.

**An app studio hits cold start roughly a dozen times a year.** That is the whole business model. So the system you need, and the system nobody sells, runs on **competitor evidence and category evidence instead of your own account data**, and hands you a ranked plan on day zero.

That is not "1000x better than AdCreative." That is a different axis entirely. It also happens to be perfectly demoable in three hours, because a brand with zero data is the easiest possible starting state to show on a screen.

### The hard constraints, so you do not promise something the APIs will not do

- **Meta Ad Library API gives commercial ad data for EU and UK audiences only** (DSA-mandated, ~12 months retention). Everywhere else it returns political and social-issue ads only. EU commercial ads give `eu_total_reach` as a single number, **no spend, no engagement, no CTR, no targeting**. The creative assets themselves are not exposed. Rate limit is roughly 200 calls/hour, tokens expire ~60 days, government-ID verification required.
- **This is why Apify is a partner and why you should use it.** Every serious competitor-intel tool (Foreplay Spyder, Atria, BigSpy, Minea) is scraping, not API-calling. *Meta v. Bright Data* (N.D. Cal., Jan 2024) held that Meta's ToS bind users, not logged-out visitors, and Meta waived appeal. Logged-out public Ad Library scraping is defensible in the US. Scraping while logged in is not.
- **Spain and Spanish are in the EU.** Your actual launch market is one of the few places the official Ad Library API returns real commercial data. That is a genuine advantage, not a coincidence, and it is worth one line in the pitch.
- **TikTok's Commercial Content Library is closed** to commercial entities. Approved researchers only.
- **Manus has a native Meta Ads Manager connector.** Also Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, Notion, Slack, Stripe, HubSpot, GitHub, Linear, monday.com, Zoom, Supabase. **No Google Ads or Shopify connector found in the docs**, so plan those as browser-driven or custom MCP.
- **Manus reliability drops hard on long chains.** Community-aggregated success rates run roughly 85% at one step and under 30% at twelve or more. Pre-run the long jobs. Keep the live demo short.

## 3. The build: what to actually make

**Working name: pick one. My recommendation is `Cold Start`.**

It names the problem, it is memorable in a pitch, and it is the one word that separates you from every competitor in the room. Alternatives: `Lever` (which lever is broken), `Kernel`, `Studio OS`.

### One line

*The marketing department for a brand that has no data yet.*

### What it does

You give it a product and a market. It does not ask for your ad account, because you do not have one.

1. **Reads the field.** Apify scrapes the Meta Ad Library for the category in the target market and language. Which advertisers are running, how long their ads have been live (longevity is the only honest proxy for what is working), what hooks and emotional frames they use, what funnel type sits behind each ad, what the landing pages look like.
2. **Reads the demand.** Reviews, app store complaints, Reddit and forum language for the actual problem, in the actual language, in the buyers' own words.
3. **Decides the format, and this is the part nobody else does.** It outputs a ranked call: quiz funnel, UGC video, static, long-form landing page, or comparison page. With the evidence for the call, and what it would take to be wrong.
4. **Builds only what it recommended.** Higgsfield renders the creative. Manus deploys the live landing page or funnel. Copy is written in the target language, not translated into it.
5. **Ships it.** Manus's native Meta Ads connector pushes the campaign structure, paused, for human approval.

### The before/after (this is what wins criterion 1)

The demo is not a hypothetical brand. It is **your own unlaunched app.**

**Before (real, on screen):** the app studio's dog separation-anxiety app. A finished GTM research pass says Spain first, Spanish, separation anxiety, guilt-relief framing, hard paywall with a 7-day trial at roughly €55 to 60. **Zero assets exist.** No landing page, no funnel, no creative, no ad account. A document and nothing else.

**After (real, on screen, live URL):** a Spanish-language quiz funnel deployed at a real address, N ad creatives rendered against competitor-derived hooks, a paused Meta campaign structure, and a one-page memo saying which format was chosen and why.

**That is the most defensible before/after in the room**, because the "before" is genuinely nothing and the "after" is genuinely live. Most teams will bring a brand that already has a website and show a nicer website.

### Why this scores on all four criteria

| Criterion | How you hit it |
|---|---|
| Transformation | Research doc to live funnel plus ad set. Nothing to something, not something to something prettier. |
| Speed | A live URL and a countable number of assets. Volume is the only honest evidence of throughput in three hours. |
| Use of Manus | Apify MCP, Higgsfield, native Meta Ads connector, browser, Wide Research across competitors, deployed output. Four surfaces, not one clever prompt. Kaan will notice. |
| Real-world utility | You are spending real money against it. Say that out loud: this funnel takes budget next week. Ipek's clause, answered directly. |

**Use Wide Research as the visual centrepiece.** Hundreds of parallel agents building a competitor and creative matrix across a whole category is Manus's most legible differentiator and it looks like nothing else on a stage. Budget for it: Wide Research alone can run 800 to 1,500+ credits and the free tier is 300 a day.
