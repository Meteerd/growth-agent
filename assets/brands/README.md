| Folder | Brand | What is inside | Visual style to match |

Rules:
- These are brand-owned images; do not upload them anywhere except the providers we already use for generation (OpenAI image references, fal) and only when needed.
- People in photos: never present them as customers, patients or reviewers; never animate a real person (e.g. the founder photo) into words they did not say. Doctors in a.clinic visuals are illustrations; keep it that way unless the operator provides consented footage.
- Hound before/after style illustrations are fine for behavior (they are not body or medical before/after). Never create body before/after imagery for a.clinic (rule RX3).
- Patient documents or app screenshots with personal data must never be added here.
